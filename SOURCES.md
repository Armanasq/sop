## Verification levels

- **Year evidence**: actual placement in the source board's year column.
- **Linked source**: an author or Notion URL is known; the destination may not have been fetched.
- **Verified statement**: the original document was fetched and confirmed to be a statement of purpose.
- **Redistributable document**: confirmed reuse/redistribution terms permit inclusion in this repository.

Avoid inferring application year solely from enrollment dates. Deduplicate canonical source URLs and keep uncertainty explicit. The 2025 index currently includes linked sources; it does not assert that external PDF bytes were fetched or that redistribution rights were obtained.

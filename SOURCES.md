## Acquisition procedure

1. Traverse the public Notion collection and read its application-year metadata.
2. Follow applicant records to linked PDF/DOCX, Google Drive, or other documents.
3. Download actual file bytes, validate file signatures, record SHA-256.
4. Store binaries under the application year and log provenance in `index.csv`.
5. Report dead, access-restricted or HTML-only links without falsely claiming their downloads succeeded.

Google Drive folder links may contain multiple SOPs per applicant; recovered variants are preserved separately.

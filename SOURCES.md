## Data-quality rules

1. Record the **application-submission year** only with explicit source evidence. Do not infer it from an institution's admission or enrollment year.
2. Deduplicate on canonical source URL; verify author identity when multiple files exist.
3. Check linked records for redirects and broken links before marking them verified.
4. Link to the canonical statement hosted by its author; do not copy full text or PDF without an applicable redistribution license or direct permission.
5. Preserve uncertainty in `year_status` instead of silently slotting records into a year.

## Current limitations

The public top-level Notion page exposes applicant names, institutions, research areas and links, but not the per-applicant application-year property in its accessible HTML. Individual Notion subpages could not be retrieved through the available browsing route. The initial index is therefore partial and its application-year verification is incomplete. It is not a full export of the database.
